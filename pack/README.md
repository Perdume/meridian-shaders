# Meridian

A shader pack for [Horizon](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon) (shader API 0.1), for Horizon Nightly 2026-11-01
and later (`meridian_compat` is the pack for Horizon 0.2.97).

Shadow-mapped sunlight with soft, coloured shadows; scattered skies with high clouds, stars, the Milky Way and an
aurora; coloured block light and glowing blocks; light through leaves; wet ground in the rain; LabPBR normal and
specular maps; clear water with waves, refraction, caustics, foam and reflections of the world; ambient occlusion;
underwater, Nether and End atmospheres; bloom, depth of field, motion blur, chromatic aberration, eye adaptation, a
filmic grade, film grain and temporal anti-aliasing.

A Quality preset (Low to Ultra) and 58 options on 8 pages of Horizon's Shader Options screen. MIT licensed.
